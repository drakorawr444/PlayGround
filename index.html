HOW TO PLAY

1
Aim and throw

Drag toward your rival and release. The longer the drag, the harder the throw.

2
Mind the wind

Wind pushes the projectile in flight. Check the meter before you commit.

3
One weapon each

Every fighter has a single weapon with its own weight, damage and range.

4
Tap after the throw

Tap the screen while your shot is airborne to trigger its ability: detonate, return, split or boost.
